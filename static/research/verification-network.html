Verification must be cheaper than trust

A weighted concept network for the claim in §7 of the review draft: that the binding constraint on oversight is the cost of checking, not the reliability of the checker. Edge colour is sign, thickness is weight, rings are distance from the claim.

Select a node

Every node is focusable. Arrow keys move between them; Enter or Space pins the detail panel to that node.

What is plotted

Vertical axis: trust in the assistant, 0 → 1. Horizontal: time on task. Cell brightness is the modelled rate at which a supervisor verifies. The model is a caricature on purpose — one learning-by-doing term, one automation-bias term, one cost term — and it exists to make a shape visible, not to be a model of anyone.

Two channels, one discontinuity

Upper trace: attention residue for a supervisor, decaying after each interruption. Lower trace: the agent's discrete completion signal. The claim is not that residue is large — it is that the supervisor pays for it in real time while the agent's cost of being interrupted is zero.
